# Controlled optimal-execution experiment

**Completed:** 20 trained policies, 400,000 training transitions, 180,224 held-out episode evaluations, and 80 saved checkpoints. Scientific execution used isolated Python **3.6.6**, NumPy **1.15.1**, and PyTorch **0.4.1 CPU**. The experiment ran on October 4, 2026 (UTC), with a scientific knowledge cutoff strictly before September 20, 2018.

The small study compares DDPG and TD3, nominal and randomized impact training, TWAP, and a fixed nominal discrete Almgren-Chriss schedule. All experiment data are generated by the stated synthetic model. No stock, market dataset, live trade, or empirical calibration was used. All reported costs are normalized simulation quantities, not dollars or basis points.

## Main result

The prespecified equal-weight average over seven nonnominal scenarios gives a TD3 randomized-minus-nominal objective difference of **-0.008446755**, or **-2.1082%** relative to the nominal group mean. Its five-seed paired 95% confidence interval is **[-0.017430751, +0.000537242]**. The interval includes zero; this experiment does not establish a systematic randomization benefit. DDPG's corresponding difference is -0.009205463 with interval [-0.038231723, +0.019820797]. Report these outcomes without selecting favorable seeds or scenarios.

In the largest joint shift, randomized TD3 has objective 0.768910791, improving on nominal TD3 (0.830912752) and fixed AC (0.975153208), while **TWAP is better at 0.734510304**. In the nominal scenario, fixed AC (0.296921638) is slightly below randomized TD3 (0.297545946). The analytical control remains a valuable comparator. Confidence intervals quantify training-seed variation conditional on the fixed common evaluation paths. See the JSON for all scenario results and limitations.

## Saved outputs

Each `runs/<algorithm>_<regime>_seed<seed>/` contains 1,000 training-episode records, four 5,000-transition checkpoints, final replay data, RNG state, runtime identity, configuration, and a hashed execution record. `evaluation/` contains every policy/scenario trade trajectory and cost component in NumPy archives, common price and liquidity paths, raw CSV, seed means, and execution record. `analysis-summary.json` includes already-computed plot series; current document-rendering tools can draw these without recomputing scientific statistics in a newer library.

## Boundaries

Training does not observe hidden impact parameters, future liquidity, or future price innovations. The final checkpoint is selected by the frozen transition budget. Risk is an explicit after-trade inventory penalty for independent price fluctuations; it is not total unconditional shortfall variance. The nominal AC recurrence is exact only for constant unit liquidity and quadratic trade cost, and its fixed schedule is feasible under the same action bounds as learned policies. All policy inputs, rewards, parameter ranges, deviations from original published benchmarks, and model omissions are specified in the protocol.
